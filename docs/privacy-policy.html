My Service Planner Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy describes how My Service Planner handles information. “Application developer,” “we,” “us” and “our” mean the developer identified in the application's Google Play listing. Privacy inquiries can be submitted through the project's contact page.

Your data belongs to you

The application developer does not collect your planner records through your use of My Service Planner. Your appointments, contact details, phone numbers, access notes, meeting notes, location records and timer logs are not sent to the application developer. We do not claim ownership of these records, sell them, or use them for advertising or profiling.

The planner works without an internet connection. It stores and processes your records locally to organize appointments, maintain visit history, provide reminders, track time and personalize your planner. There is no online planner account with the application developer and no developer-operated service that stores your planner records.

You decide whether to export information, save a backup with a provider, or authorize another application to access supported information. These choices can give the selected recipient access; they do not send a copy to the application developer.

Information used on your device

Depending on the features you use, local information can include names, addresses, selected phone numbers and their labels, appointment schedules and history, access and meeting notes, timer records and labels, location readings, preferences, branding images and text, and profile details such as a name and birthday month and day. Record and device identifiers help keep your files and recovery information consistent. They do not create an account with the application developer.

When you choose a phone number through Android's contact picker and save it, the app stores that selected number and its label with your planner contact. It does not scan your address book or maintain a live connection to the phone contact. You can replace or remove the saved number. Your backups can include saved numbers, just as they include other planner records.

When you press Text or Call, the app passes the selected number to your chosen messaging or calling app. My Service Planner does not send the message or place the call itself, and it does not read your messages or call history. The message body starts empty. Your calling and messaging app preferences stay on that device and are not included in planner backups.

If you enable location features and grant permission, the app uses location to help recognize appointment arrivals and record visit-related locations. These records remain local unless included in information you choose to export or transfer. You can revoke location permission in Android settings; this stops further permitted access but does not erase previously saved records.

Android's notification and alarm facilities deliver reminders you configure. File and image selection gives the app access to items or destinations you choose. Device authentication uses Android's security facilities; My Service Planner does not receive your device PIN, password or biometric templates.

Backups, transfers and encryption

Saving a local planner change does not automatically upload it to a cloud service. The app disables Android automatic backup for its planner data. You control the app's backup, export and restore actions.

A backup can include planner files, related location records and an index for recovery and reconciliation. If you choose Google Drive or another storage provider as a destination, that provider receives the selected files and handles them under its own policies. An installed document provider can also supply a file you choose to restore. The application developer does not receive these files or access your storage account through these actions.

File encryption is optional. Files retain their selected encryption state when backed up. Recovery words and encryption keys are handled on your devices; the application developer has no recovery-key service and cannot recover your words or decrypt your planner for you. File names and index metadata can remain readable even when file contents are encrypted. Encryption of a new file does not encrypt older copies elsewhere.

Printing, exporting, copying text, transferring files to another device or sharing a file gives the destination or recipient access to the information you select. The recipient may retain its own copy. Removing information locally does not remove those other copies.

Other apps and services you choose

Navigate passes the chosen destination to a mapping app or service. Searching a fun fact sends that fact's public search query to a compatible app or opens its citation. Reading the bundled facts does not require an online search. Calling, messaging, mapping, search and storage providers handle your subsequent interactions under their own policies.

If you authorize a compatible AI assistant, that assistant can access the planner information and operations permitted by the connection. Information you provide to the assistant may be processed by its provider, including on that provider's servers. The same applies if you copy planner information into a chat service. The application developer does not operate these assistant services or receive a copy of your conversations through the planner.

Google Play and purchases

Google independently processes information about Google Play and its services, including downloads, purchases, device information and applicable diagnostic or usage information, under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may make store, performance, order and refund information available to the application developer. This platform information is separate from your planner records and does not give the developer access to them.

Google Play handles payments for the optional Branding unlock. My Service Planner uses purchase status, signed receipt information and purchase tokens to verify, acknowledge and restore the unlock. It keeps receipt information locally. The application developer does not receive the payment-card details you enter into Google Play. Google maintains its transaction records; available purchase and refund information may be used by the application developer to provide purchase support and meet applicable business obligations.

Contact and support information

If you contact the application developer, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your account name, reply address, message and attachments. This is voluntary support communication, not automatic collection of your planner records. We use it to answer your inquiry, investigate the reported issue or administer a purchase-related request.

The GitHub contact page is a public issue form. Other people can read submitted issues. Use a general description or a redacted example; do not post planner databases, customer or patient information, recovery words, payment details or other confidential information. You may also use the developer contact information shown in the app's Google Play listing when available.

GitHub hosts this policy and the issue form and processes visits, accounts and submissions under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Merely viewing this policy does not give GitHub or the application developer access to your planner records.

Retention and deletion

Your local records remain under your control. Removing an appointment or contact from a view may retain history or removal markers in the planner file. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its private local storage; exported files, files in shared storage, backups and copies on other devices must be removed separately at their destinations. Removing app storage can also remove encryption keys, so keep recovery access for encrypted files you intend to retain.

The application developer has no server copy of your planner records to retrieve or delete. For information you voluntarily send us, we retain it only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the issue, maintain necessary purchase or dispute records, or satisfy applicable legal obligations. You can use the contact mechanism above to request correction or deletion of information you provided, without posting confidential details publicly. Any continuing retention required for those purposes will be explained. Removing information from our control does not necessarily remove public copies or records retained independently by GitHub, Google or another provider under its own policies.

Security and changes to this policy

My Service Planner uses local storage, Android security facilities and optional encryption to help protect your information. You control access to your device, recovery words and selected backup accounts. No storage or transfer method can guarantee protection in every circumstance.

We will update this policy when the app's information-handling practices change and revise the effective date above. Where a change requires your consent, that consent will be requested before the new use begins.
