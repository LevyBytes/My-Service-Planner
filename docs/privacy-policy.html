My Service Planner Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how My Service Planner handles information. “Application developer,” “we,” “us” and “our” mean the developer identified in the application's Google Play listing. Privacy inquiries can be sent to privacy@myserviceplanner.download.

Your data belongs to you

Your planner data is yours. The application developer does not collect it, receive it or keep it, and keeps no records about the people who use the app. Your appointments, contact details, phone numbers, access notes, meeting notes, location records and timer logs are not sent to the application developer. We do not claim ownership of these records, sell them, or use them for advertising or profiling.

The planner works without an internet connection. It stores and processes your records locally to organize appointments, maintain visit history, provide reminders, track time and personalize your planner. There is no online planner account with the application developer and no developer-operated service that stores your planner records.

Who else sees any of your data is your choice alone. It reaches someone else only when you send it there: an app or service you pick in My Service Planner (a texting, calling or mapping app, a storage provider or an assistant), a file you export, print, share or back up, or a device you link. Each receives only what you give it and handles it under its own policies; the application developer receives no copy through any of them.

Information used on your device

Depending on the features you use, local information can include names, addresses, selected phone numbers and their labels, appointment schedules and history, access and meeting notes, timer records and labels, location readings, the map coordinates of addresses, preferences, branding images and text, and profile details such as a name and birthday month and day. Record and device identifiers help keep your files and recovery information consistent. They do not create an account with the application developer.

When you choose a phone number through Android's contact picker and save it, the app stores that selected number and its label with your planner contact. It does not scan your address book or maintain a live connection to the phone contact. You can replace or remove the saved number. Your backups can include saved numbers, just as they include other planner records.

When you press Text or Call, the app passes the selected number to the messaging or calling app you choose in Planner Settings. Text also copies the number to the clipboard, so you can paste it into a messaging app that does not take it. My Service Planner does not send the message or place the call itself, and it does not read your messages or call history. The message body starts empty. Your calling and messaging app preferences stay on that device and are not included in planner backups.

If you enable location features and grant permission, the app uses location to help recognize appointment arrivals and record visit-related locations. To recognize an arrival, the app asks Android for the map coordinates of a visit's address and saves them with the visit and its contact; on phones with Google Play services, Android looks the address up with Google, under the Google Privacy Policy. These records remain local unless included in information you choose to export or transfer. You can revoke location permission in Android settings; this stops further permitted access but does not erase previously saved records.

Android's notification and alarm facilities deliver the reminders you configure and show Arrival Assistance's notification while it records; the app asks for notification permission when one of them first needs it. File and image selection gives the app access to items or destinations you choose. Device authentication uses Android's security facilities; My Service Planner does not receive your device PIN, password or biometric templates.

Backups, transfers and encryption

Saving a local planner change does not automatically upload it to a cloud service. The app disables Android automatic backup for its planner data. You control the app's backup, export and restore actions.

A backup can include planner files, related location records and an index for recovery and reconciliation. If you choose Google Drive or another storage provider as a destination, that provider receives the selected files and handles them under its own policies. An installed document provider can also supply a file you choose to restore. The application developer does not receive these files or access your storage account through these actions.

Linked Devices keeps your planner the same on devices you link. They exchange it directly over Bluetooth, encrypted between the two devices, never through the application developer or the internet.

File encryption is optional. Files retain their selected encryption state when backed up. Recovery words and encryption keys are handled on your devices; the application developer has no recovery-key service and cannot recover your words or decrypt your planner for you. File names and index metadata can remain readable even when file contents are encrypted. Encryption of a new file does not encrypt older copies elsewhere.

Printing, exporting, copying text, transferring files to another device or sharing a file gives the destination or recipient access to the information you select. The recipient may retain its own copy. Removing information locally does not remove those other copies.

Other apps and services you choose

Navigate hands the visit's address to Google Maps, in the Maps app or at google.com/maps in your browser, under the Google Privacy Policy. Searching a fun fact sends that fact's public search query to a compatible app or opens its citation. Reading the bundled facts does not require an online search. Calling, messaging, mapping, search and storage providers handle your subsequent interactions under their own policies.

If you authorize a compatible AI assistant, that assistant can access the planner information and operations permitted by the connection. Information you provide to the assistant may be processed by its provider, including on that provider's servers. The same applies if you copy planner information into a chat service. The application developer does not operate these assistant services or receive a copy of your conversations through the planner.

Google Play and purchases

Google independently processes information about Google Play and its services, including downloads, purchases, device information and applicable diagnostic or usage information, under the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play's billing library, which the app uses for the Branding unlock, sends Google its own logs under that policy. Google Play Console shows the application developer reports about the app, such as install counts, crash reports and orders; they come from Google, not from your planner, and the developer keeps no records about you from them. This platform information does not give the developer access to your planner records.

The Branding unlock is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google shows the price, takes the payment and keeps the record of the purchase; the application developer never sees your payment details. Play then gives the app a signed receipt, which the app checks, acknowledges and keeps on your device to unlock branding. Restore Purchase asks Play for it again after a reinstall or on another device signed in to the same Google account. Refunds go through Google Play. If you ask the application developer for one, the developer finds your order in Google Play Console by its order number or your email address and refunds it there. The application developer keeps no copy of your order.

Contact and support information

If you contact the application developer, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message and attachments. This is voluntary support communication, not automatic collection of your planner records. We use it only to reply to you.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to privacy@myserviceplanner.download. Use a general description or a redacted example; do not send planner databases, customer or patient information, recovery words, payment details or other confidential information. You may also use the developer contact information shown in the app's Google Play listing when available.

GitHub hosts this policy and processes visits under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Merely viewing this policy does not give GitHub or the application developer access to your planner records.

Retention and deletion

Your local records remain under your control. Removing an appointment or contact from a view may retain history or removal markers in the planner file. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its private local storage; exported files, files in shared storage, backups and copies on other devices must be removed separately at their destinations. Removing app storage can also remove encryption keys, so keep recovery access for encrypted files you intend to retain.

The application developer has no server copy of your planner records to retrieve or delete, and keeps no records about you. An email you send is used only to reply to you, and it is deleted when you ask. Google and the other services you choose keep their own records under their own policies.

Security and changes to this policy

My Service Planner uses local storage, Android security facilities and optional encryption to help protect your information. You control access to your device, recovery words and selected backup accounts. No storage or transfer method can guarantee protection in every circumstance.

We will update this policy when the app's information-handling practices change and revise the effective date above. Where a change requires your consent, that consent will be requested before the new use begins.
